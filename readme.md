I operate at the intersection of **Advanced Artificial Intelligence, E-commerce Architecture, and Digital Publishing**. My objective is to engineer scalable digital infrastructures that automate complex processes, from deep tech integrations to localized financial ecosystems.

> **Role:** Founder & Architect
> Architecting a specialized digital publishing house and exclusive literary community. Overseeing the complete digital infrastructure, from domain architecture to high-end book distribution and community management.

#### 🧠 AI Prototyping & Intelligence
